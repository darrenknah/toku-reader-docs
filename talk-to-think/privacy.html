Talk to Think — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Talk to Think does not collect, store, or share any information about you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your recordings, your words, and everything you paste stay on your iPhone. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone. Sorting runs on your iPhone using fixed rules. Where your iPhone has Apple Intelligence turned on, it may be used on the device to suggest names for subjects.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, unless you choose to send it. When you press Copy or Share, the text goes wherever you send it, such as Notes, Mail or Messages. The app itself has no servers.

The first time you record, your iPhone may download Apple's on-device speech model from Apple. That download is handled by iOS, not by this app, and sends none of your words.

What the app does not have

No accounts. No sign-in. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking.

The usage log

The app keeps a small count of things such as how many notes you made. It stays on your iPhone and is only ever copied if you press Copy log yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete a note in the app and it is gone. Delete the app and everything it stored is deleted with it.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
